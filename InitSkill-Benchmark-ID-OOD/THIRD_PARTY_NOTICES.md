# Third-party sources

`references/init_benchmark_v1/upstream/metatopia` is the Bessa Research Group's Metatopia source at commit `b30ccbfdb7f9648e63cc087e2e41091a383d1887`, distributed with its original BSD3-Clause license and notices.

`references/init_benchmark_v1/upstream/Keras-GAN` is Erik Linder-Norén's Keras-GAN source at commit `3ff3be4b4b2fa338b18e469888b6f0b7a1b2db48`, distributed with its original license and notices. It is a general GAN reference, not a verified full Yu topology-optimization training repository.

Paper metadata and original download URLs are included. PDFs are downloaded on demand from the recorded author/publisher URLs rather than embedded in the GitHub bundle. Runtime source checks verify the bundled upstream files; missing optional PDFs do not block computation.

NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch, JAX, CVXOPT and other dependencies retain their own licenses. They are installed from their distributions and are not bundled as environments or binary wheels here.
